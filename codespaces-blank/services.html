Adoption Process

Meet and greet

The firs thing we do is put the animal in a controlled setting with the person interested in adopting. So we can see how each interact to see if they would be a good fit. Paperwork

Depending on the animal there is paperwork to fill out along with processing and rehoming fees ( Depending on your income level we may be able to waive the fee) Goodbye and Hello

After everything looks good it is now time for us to say goodbye and for you to say hello to your new family member.

Foster program

We always love when our volunteers are ready to foster a new animal. 40% of the time they actually adopt the animal

Requirements

The first thing we must do is send someone out to do an onsite visit of your property. Once we are at the property we have you fill out some paperwork along with getting to know you and who also lives in the home such as your human family members along with what other animals you have

Once we have everything in order we put you in our database and when we find a match with one of our rescues we contact you. Dont fret if you dont get an animal right away we have a lot of awesome volunters in the list already

Volunteer Role Descriptions

These are not paid postions

Foster Care - The animal lives on your property for a designated time or until an adoption occurs.

Bathing Animals - You will give a bath and clean up specific animals that we have available.

Walking animals - You will take animals on a Walking

Play time - You will give the animals exorcising which is a bit different then just talking them for a Walking

Transportation - Sometimes we dont have enough vehicles which is why we need you to help transport them.
