Welcome To Twin Cities Animal Rescue

First off we want to welcome you to our community nonprofit animal rescue.

Our Mission

We want to be able to take in any rescue that people bring us or that we find ourselves. We believe every creature deserves a chance at life

Impact Statistics

  • 1 in 3 animals saved by Twin Cities Animal Rescue last year
  • 40% improvement over last year finding animals new homes
  • Funded new animal building with community donations
  • Tours for Special needs children paid by community
