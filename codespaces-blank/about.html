Over 9000 Animals

That is where we are now. We helped over 9000 animals in the past 5 years of being open.



Our employees

We have 35 full time staff and over 100 volunteers who help us whenever they can and that number keeps growing. At anytime our rescue has anywhere from 100 to 500 animals at all times.

Core Values

We have 5 core values that we live by everyday. Every animal deserves a chance. We are open 24/7. No animal is too small or to big to be helped. We have a meal for every animal we take in and lastly no matter who you are we have an animal for you.
